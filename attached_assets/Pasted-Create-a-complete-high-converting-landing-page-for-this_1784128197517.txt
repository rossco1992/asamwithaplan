### Idea Overview
**Product/Service**: Wedding planner app for couples doing it themselves
**Summary**: Skipping the wedding planner saves the $5,000 it would cost and creates a second job: roughly 200 decisions across six months. Which photographer earns the price. When invitations go out. Whether a florist's quote is fair or the budget is about to break. Checklists organize the tasks. Judgment on the money stays with two people doing this for the first time.

Marrymap generates a full planning timeline from the wedding date and venue, then breaks it into weekly steps. Vendor inquiry emails draft in the couple's own voice. Competing quotes line up side by side, with flags on any item priced outside the typical range. Budget tracking connects to bank data and catches overruns before they compound. One dashboard shows what is done, what comes next, and what sits at risk, so couples approve and adjust while the coordination runs underneath.

Version one covers timeline generation and vendor communication. Email integrations draft and track messages to florists, caterers, and photographers. Couples drop in the quotes that come back, and anything priced past the budget surfaces on its own. First ten couples reveal which decisions spike the sharpest anxiety, and those automate first. Day-of scheduling and seating layout wait until core planning proves out and couples trust the tool with real vendor money.

Pricing runs $29 a month for a straightforward wedding and $99 for a large, complex one, against the planner's fee they skipped. Couples find it in the big wedding-planning communities, and referral is built into the calendar: each wedding sends three newly engaged friends in behind it. Venue partnerships and vendor commissions add a second revenue line. Couples who trusted it through their own wedding hand it to friends the week those friends get engaged.
**Why Now**: The wedding planning industry is currently witnessing a transformative phase marked by significant growth and technological advancements. As of 2025, the global wedding planning market is valued at approximately $261.9 billion, expected to surge to between $500 billion and $755 billion by 2035. This growth is paralleled by a sharp increase in DIY wedding planning, driven by the financial strains of traditional wedding costs, which average $36,000 globally. With AI adoption among engaged couples nearing 36%, there's an evident shift toward utilizing technology for complex planning tasks, highlighting the opportune moment for launching an AI-driven wedding planning solution. 

Technological advancements have enabled the creation of AI solutions that can handle natural-language planning workflows, a crucial development given the standardization of wedding planning tasks across timelines, budgets, and vendor interactions. Foundation models and sophisticated SaaS integrations allow for seamless automation of various planning processes, from vendor communication to quote evaluation. This offers a substantial edge over traditional methods, where complexity and vendor-side constraints often hinder effective planning. 

Social and regulatory factors also play a crucial role in shaping this opportunity. The increasing demand for hyper-personalized experiences and shared decision-making reinforces the preference for accessible, AI-based planning tools. Additionally, the regulatory environment, while not specifically tailored to weddings, emphasizes transparency and data protection, offering a chance to build trust and differentiate from older platforms that may not align with current privacy standards.
